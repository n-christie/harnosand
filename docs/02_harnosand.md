## 2021

### July 2021

Härnösand Municipality, in collaboration with Region Västernorrland and six other municipalities, launched the 'Good and Close Care' program. This initiative aims to support the development of health, care, and welfare services in the region

https://harnosand.se/stod--omsorg/god-och-nara-vard.html


Reports indicated a significant rise in the employment of temporary staff within elderly care services. In the first five months of 2021, the municipality spent 6.8 million SEK on temporary personnel, surpassing the total expenditure for the entire previous year by 2.5 million SEK

https://www.allehanda.se/2021-07-12/jatteokning-av-stafettpersonal-i-harnosand-aldreomsorgen-spenderar-miljoner-en-jobbig-svacka/?utm_source=chatgpt.com

## 2023 

### March 2023 
Ivo - the Health and Social Care Inspectorate conducted inspections of elderly care facilities in Härnösand, identifying areas needing improvement, particularly concerning staff competencies in medical assessments. The municipality was required to present corrective action plans by mid-May 2023.

https://www.sverigesradio.se/artikel/varden-pa-aldreboenden-i-harnosand-far-skarp-kritik-fran-ivo?

### November 2023

Härnösand Municipality announced plans to increase the baseline staffing within elderly care. This initiative aims to ensure adequate care provision, convert temporary positions to permanent roles, and secure a stable workforce for the future.

https://www.nordsverige.se/2023-11-13/harnosand-utokar-grundbemanningen-inom-aldreomsorgen-5e562?

## 2024 

### April 2024

In April 2024, Utfallsfonden committed up to 50 million SEK over five years to support projects aimed at improving the health and quality of life for the elderly in Härnösand. This investment focuses on preventive measures to help seniors maintain independence and reduce the need for extensive care services.

https://mittharnosand.se/harnosand-vaxer/utvecklingsprojekt/skola-och-omsorg/nytt-aldreboende

### October 2024

In October 2024, the municipality approved the development of a new dementia care home near Jensenvägen. This facility aims to meet the increasing demand for specialized dementia care and is part of a broader strategy to enhance elderly care services in the region.

https://mittharnosand.se/harnosand-vaxer/utvecklingsprojekt/skola-och-omsorg/nytt-aldreboende
